This prerelease preserves the separately audited shifted-cut diagnostic against validation04. It is not the v4.0 release or the 68-fit benchmark.

Both one-hour synthetic captured linearizations passed fresh MAP, covariance, residual-energy and deterministic-equation checks: 24 proposal solves and 8 undamped covariance solves, with no physical propagation or nuisance refit. The largest MAP discrepancy was 2.2442e-12 posterior standard deviations; the largest relative covariance discrepancy was 9.1396e-14.

The 1,800-second offsets provide genuine interior-parameter movement for both methods and segment periods. The 300-second pulse offsets provide no cut-coverage evidence; their numerical agreement remains reported. These checks do not establish multi-day or flight equivalence, accepted-fit covariance, a speedup, or release readiness. The benchmark remains 0/68 in this dated diagnostic record; the original v3.1 campaign remains separate.
