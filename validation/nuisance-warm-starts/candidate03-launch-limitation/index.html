Warm-start validation: launch limitation

The first profile experiment and diagnostic replays did not use the production backend’s QR datum path at process startup. Their failures are preserved observations; they do not establish production warm-start behavior. Scientific acceptance, covariance authority and whole-fit admission remain false.

The isolated launcher omitted ORBIT_POC_NUMERICS_QR_PREFIX and ORBIT_POC_NUMERICS_SOLVER before native startup. GROOPS initializes nuisance parameter datums before the profile processing step enables those variables. The production backend supplies them before process launch.

  • The original 27 attempts remain: three disabled cold runs completed, 15 enabled exports failed, and nine warm dependents were blocked. All 15 comparisons rejected.
  • Four diagnostic replays identified the first refresh difference as a code-bias coordinate changing from negative zero to positive zero. This does not prove there were no other differences.
  • The original prior fixture created no native code/TEC constraint rows. A separate pilot with explicit transmitter-bias selection failed during legacy 5×5 eigendecomposition before QR profiling. Both failed fixtures are retained.
  • The next experiment requires the production prelaunch QR controls, actual launch-environment evidence and native datum artifacts. Signed-zero encoding is being corrected separately, without an epsilon tolerance or altered convergence limits.

The frozen v4 campaign, original v3.1 benchmark and production backend are unchanged by these harness repairs. See the preserved original report and draft PR #7.
