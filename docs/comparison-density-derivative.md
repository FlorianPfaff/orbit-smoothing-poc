# Propagated density sensitivity diagnostic

The native density sensitivity uses the same low-degree gravity-only state
transition as the reduced-dynamic estimator. It omits the full drag position and
velocity gradients, other force gradients, and geometry-dependent atmospheric
event-time sensitivities. An exact direct derivative of drag with respect to a
log-density knot does not remove these transition approximations.

Run the independent trajectory check with a retained, converged April development
density fit and the executable used for the intended diagnostic:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/check_density_trajectory_derivative.py \
  --binary /path/to/groops \
  --result /path/to/development-density-fit/result.json \
  --output work/native-validation/density-derivative \
  --date 2024-04-24 --knot-hours 6 --step 0.05 \
  --closure-position-m 2e-6 --closure-velocity-m-s 3e-9
```

The script verifies the source fit's retained artifact hashes and freezes the
executable. It fixes the physical initial six-state and independently propagates
the nominal trajectory and four perturbations, at knot displacements of ±0.05
and ±0.025. Each branch repeatedly reevaluates every physical force on its own
updated reference until successive trajectories agree within the specified
diagnostic closure limits. The propagation uses no density design columns.

The script then extracts the native density column at the closed nominal
reference. It compares this with central differences at both steps and their
Richardson extrapolation. It reports step sensitivity, the last-iteration closure
change divided by the perturbation, and finite-difference values before the
knot's support. These quantify observed numerical limitations; the closure proxy
is not a certified error bound. Output includes raw derivative arrays, per-branch
closure records, native configurations, command records, and executable hashes.

The [current UTC-day diagnostic](../validation/comparison-v3/density-trajectory-derivative-74863.json)
uses the campaign binary `74863d5a…`, the complete April 24 UTC day, and a single
six-hour knot. Its full force model includes the selected 1-second atmospheric
quadrature and 0.1-second thermal maximum step. The native position derivative
differs from the extrapolated finite difference by **0.648 mm per unit
log-density**, or **0.0267%** in maximum norm. The response is 2.432 m per unit
log-density. Step halving changes the derivative by 0.357 mm; the Richardson
correction is 0.119 mm, the closure-change proxy is 21.1 µm, and the observed
pre-support floor is 6.31 µm. The velocity discrepancy is 0.0276%. All five
nonlinear branches close: two iterations for the nominal trajectory and three
for each perturbation.

A separate [mean-trajectory closure check](../validation/comparison-v3/density-fit-closure-74863.json)
compares the converged fitted orbit with this independently propagated nominal
trajectory at all 17,281 exact native epochs. The maximum difference is
0.1023 µm in position and 0.1084 nm/s in velocity. This checks the fitted mean
under the same force model; it does not establish covariance calibration.

The source fit and the diagnostic have distinct, recorded Python revisions;
their native executable is identical. These checks quantify one development
knot and one fitted mean, with no global trajectory-Jacobian error bound or
claim about scientific orbit accuracy. Raw artifacts are retained under the
locators in the compact records, outside Git.

The [earlier April diagnostic](../validation/comparison-v3/density-trajectory-derivative-274b.json)
used binary `274bd986…`, a GPS-midnight engineering arc, and a single six-hour
knot. The native position derivative differed from the extrapolated finite
difference by **0.710 mm per unit log-density**, or **0.0267%** in maximum norm.
The finite-difference response was 2.660 m per unit log-density. Step halving
changed the derivative by 0.391 mm; the Richardson correction was 0.130 mm, the
closure-change proxy was 17.6 µm, and the observed pre-support floor was 4.70 µm.
The velocity derivative's relative discrepancy was 0.0276%. All five nonlinear
branches closed in three iterations.

This demonstrates a small, measurable transition approximation for that knot.
It does not validate all knots, later binaries, production UTC-day arcs, or
posterior covariance. The independent dense atmospheric force mesh was added
after this executable. The earlier
[inconclusive precision attempt](../validation/comparison-v3/density-trajectory-precision-attempt.json)
is preserved: its requested 0.2 µm closure was below an observed roughly 1 µm
floor. Changing the diagnostic closure limit did not relax any estimator or
production acceptance threshold.

Raw files use the locator `raw-artifacts/density-trajectory-fd-local-events`;
they are generated by the command above and are not bundled in the curated
repository. The compact record preserves their content hashes.
